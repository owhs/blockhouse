## Findings

**R13-1 · 6, 10, 11, 1 · major · both sites · `#/` and any list at 390×844** Resize, dispatch `resize`, reload. The shell now fits, but the content does not: on the dashboard `.main` has `scrollWidth 558` inside a 390 px window, so the whole page — heading included — scrolls sideways. `.card.attn-card` is 361 px wide with `scrollWidth 542`; each `.row-item` inside it measures **535 px** (`.row-label` 351, `.chip stale` 122), so every "Continue editing" title is cut at the card edge and all five status chips sit entirely off screen (screenshot taken). On `#/c/product` the table reaches x 709 in a 390 px window. Cause is the same family as R11-1/R12-1 one level lower: `.attn-card` (`studio.css:1297`) is `display: grid` with no column track, so its implicit column takes the row's max-content, and `.attn-card .row-item` (`:1303`) is `width: 100%` with the default `min-width: auto`. Fix: `grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr)` on `.attn-card`, `min-width: 0` on `.attn-card .row-item`, and give the list table its own `overflow-x: auto` wrapper so the page heading and filters stop travelling with it.

**R13-2 · 1, 10, 12 · minor · both sites · topbar at 390×844** The topbar children shrink past their icons and collide: the ⌘K button runs `261→284` while "View site" starts at `283`, and the magnifier glyph itself extends to `285` — visibly sliced in the screenshot. `studio.css:2044–2046` gave the groups `min-width: 0` but nothing sets `flex-shrink: 0` on the icon-only controls. Add `flex: 0 0 auto` to `.searchish` and `.view-site` (the `@media (max-width: 600px)` block at `:2047` already hides their labels), or drop the search button below 600 px since ⌘K still works.

**R13-3 · 9, 4 · blocker · strongman · `#/e/d-helmsdale`** Open a published document, Publish (or Republish) and accept the dialog — "Published — it is live now", correct. Now type one character in the title and press Save. The save 409s and a modal announces *"Edited somewhere else — A newer version was saved just now — from another tab or another person."* No one else touched it. `editor.js:1471–1477` (the "Publish now" handler) does `doc.status = r.doc.status; stale = false` but never refreshes `doc.version`/`baseVersion`, so the publish's own version bump looks like a stranger's write on the very next save. Reproduced three times; a document that had not been published in the session saved cleanly, which isolates it to the publish path. The persona publishes, spots a typo, fixes it, and is told a colleague is overwriting them. Fix: assign `baseVersion = doc.version = r.doc.version` in that handler. (The conflict dialog itself is excellent — "Nothing is thrown away either way", not dismissable, Take theirs / Keep mine — it is simply being fired for nothing.)

**R13-4 · 4, 6 · major · strongman · `#/e/d-helmsdale`** After Publish the toolbar button reads "Published". Edit the document and Save: `.doc-status` correctly becomes "Published · edited", but the button still reads "Published" and there is no "Republish" anywhere — the only way to publish the new change is to reload the page (after F5 the button is "Republish"). `editor.js:107–120` updates `stale = r.stale` and calls `renderStatus()` but not `renderActions()`, which owns that button. Same gap after "Keep mine" resolves a conflict. Fix: call `renderActions()` alongside `renderStatus()` in `save()`.

**R13-5 · 8 · major · both sites · `#/plugins`** Switching Analytics on raises the toast *"Switched on. It starts working the next time the site is restarted — ask whoever hosts your site, or run `blockhouse serve` again."* (`views.js:815–816`, and the off variant on the next line). A backticked shell command is exactly the developer jargon the rubric names for this page. Worse, the toast is the only trace: after a reload the card shows the switch on with nothing saying the app is not running yet, no Analytics entry appears in the rail, and the `?` sheet ("Switch optional extras on and off for your site") never mentions a restart. Fix: drop the command, keep "ask whoever hosts your site", and mark the card "on — starts after the site restarts" until it is actually loaded.

**R13-6 · 8, 9 · major · strongman · `#/audit`** In twenty minutes I created an account, disabled it, switched an app on and off, and published a document four times. Activity, with Who = Everyone, What = Everything, When = All time, shows **one** row: "Changed an account — its role is now author, and signed it out everywhere". I never changed a role — the Sign-in toggle sends `{ active: false }`, and `audit.js:39` renders it from `e.role`, which is just the account's unchanged role. The bigger problem is the silence: `audit()` is called in fifteen places in `src/server/api.ts`, none of them for content saves or publishes, account creation (`:635` emits an event only) or plugin switches — so the "Content" filter can never match anything, while the rail says "Who changed what, and when" and the help says "A dated record of every change anyone made". Fix: audit `content.published`, `content.saved`, `user.created` and the plugin toggle, and make `user.updated` say what actually changed.

**R13-7 · 12, 8 · minor · both sites · People → ＋ Invite** The "Temporary password" field has no accessible name: `views.js:705` renders `h('div.field', {}, h('label', {}, …), pwInput)` — the label has no `for` and does not wrap the input, and the input has no id. The Email and Name fields in the same dialog are correctly bound through `renderFields` (`label[for=fld-15/16]`), so a screen-reader user meets "Email", "Name", then an anonymous edit box holding the only copy of the password. The "Role" label is likewise unbound to its radio group. Fix: route both through `renderFields`, or give the input an id and the label a `for`.

**R13-8 · 8, 9 · minor · both sites · People, and the editor's More menu** One account state has three names: the row chip says "Can sign in" / "Blocked" (`views.js:626`), the toggle is labelled "Sign-in allowed for …" (`:623`), and the toast says "Account disabled — sessions revoked" (`:624`) — "revoked" and "sessions" are both developer words. Pick one: "Can sign in" / "Cannot sign in", and "Signed them out of the studio". In the same voice, `editor.js:1530` toasts "Unpublished — the page now 404s for visitors"; the persona does not know what a 404 is — "visitors will see a page-not-found message".
